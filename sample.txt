# Telecom Egypt Bill Payment Methods

There are several ways to pay your Telecom Egypt (WE) bill:

## 1. Online Payment
- Visit the official WE website at te.eg
- Log in to your account
- Navigate to the billing section
- Enter your payment details and confirm

## 2. Mobile App
- Download the WE Pay app from the App Store or Google Play
- Register or log in
- Select "Pay Bill"
- Choose your payment method and confirm

## 3. Fawry Services
- Visit any Fawry outlet
- Provide your WE account number
- Pay the amount in cash

## 4. Bank Transfer
- Transfer the amount to WE's bank account
- Include your account number as reference
- Allow 1-2 business days for processing

## 5. ATM Payment
- Use any ATM with bill payment feature
- Select Telecom Egypt as the service provider
- Enter your account number and payment amount

## 6. WE Stores
- Visit any Telecom Egypt branch
- Pay your bill in person
- Available at all governorates

## Contact Information
For help with bill payment:
- Customer Service: 166
- Website: te.eg
